How to play

  • Bridges, vines & zip lines carry you forward.
  • Snakes swallow you and spit you out at their tail. Every few turns a snake slithers to a new spot.
  • Golden mango: roll again.
  • Shield charm blocks the next snake bite.
  • Quicksand: you miss your next turn (and lose any bonus roll).
  • River: the current washes you back 3 tiles.
  • Roll a 6 to go again. Land exactly on 100 to win; extra steps bounce you back.

Aqua wins!
